#SAMPLEPREP
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	Serum samples (100µl) should be properly thawed and spin at 5K rpm for one
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	minute, added with 100 % MeOH (300µl, in1:3 ratio) and vortex well (30s),
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	incubated on ice for 10 minutes and then centrifuged at 10K rpm for 10 minutes
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	at 4℃. Equally, transfer the supernatant into two different well-labeled tubes
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	and then speed vacuum dry and stored at -80℃. One set of the tubes is used for
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	the analysis of polar metabolites and the other one is used for the non-polar
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	metabolites. For the reconstitution of polar metabolites, 50% ACN (50 µl) is
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	added into the vacuum dried sample and for nonpolar 15% of MeOH (50 µl) is
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	used. After the addition of reconstitutes, vortex well for 10 min and
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	centrifuged at 10K rpm for 10 minutes at 4℃ and transfer the supernatant
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	(approx. 40-45 µl) into MS vials. Remove 10µl form every sample containing MS
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	vials and pooled together into a 15 ml tube to use as QC. Then, the samples were
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	stored at -80℃ for further analysis.
