#PROJECT
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	Large-scale enzyme-based xenobiotic identification for exposomics
PR:PROJECT_TYPE                  	Xenobiotic Metabolism
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	Exposomics methods are limited by low abundance of xenobiotic metabolites and
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	lack of authentic standards, which precludes identification using solely mass
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	spectrometry-based criteria. Here, we validate a method for enzymatic generation
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	of xenobiotic metabolites for use with high-resolution mass spectrometry for
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	chemical identification. Generated xenobiotic metabolites were used to confirm
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	identities of respective metabolites in mice and human samples based upon
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	accurate mass, retention time, and co-occurrence with related xenobiotic
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	metabolites. The data shared here are high-resolution Orbitrap MS data for S9
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	incubations of 140 xenobiotic compounds with 0 and 24 hour time points for all
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	reactions.
