#PROJECT
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	LC-HRMS based plasma metabolomics analysis for biomarker discovery of
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	neuroblastoma: 3-O-methyldopa is a new biomarker of poor prognosis of metastatic
PR:PROJECT_TITLE                 	disease
PR:PROJECT_TYPE                  	Biomarker Discovery
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	Neuroblastoma (NB) is the most common extra-cranial malignant tumor in children.
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	Although the survival rate of NB has improved over the years, the outcome of NB
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	still remains unfavorable in a high percentage of cases. Prognosis is currently
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	based on a combination of clinical, histo-pathological and biological features,
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	on which patients are classified in different risk groups and addressed to
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	different treatment protocols. A more accurate risk stratification remains a key
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	point in the study of NB: in particular, the availability of novel prognostic
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	biomarkers of metastatic “high risk” NB at diagnosis could help in improving
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	patient stratification, accurately predicting outcome, relapse or response to
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	treatments and also reducing unnecessary therapies and related toxicities. In
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	this study an HRMS-based approach was applied for the first time to study NB
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	with a goal of developing prognostic biomarkers that could help in improving
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	patient stratification and providing novel therapeutic targets. Starting from an
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	untargeted approach the differences in the metabolomic profiles of localized and
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	metastatic patients were investigated. Key metabolites of metastatic NB were
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	identified through differential expression analysis. Among the metabolites of
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	L-DOPA degradation pathway 3-o-methyldopa (3-O-MD) was selected and analysed in
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	a second cohort of patients using a targeted approach based on liquid
PR:PROJECT_SUMMARY               	chromatography coupled to tandem mass spectrometry (LC-MS/MS).

SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	prepared by mixing equal volumes of all the NB plasma samples. In addition, a
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	procedural blank, used to monitor contamination acquired during all stages of
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	sample preparation, and external quality control (EQC) samples were included in
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	the study and were prepared in the same way as the study samples. To avoid bias
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	due to instrument drift, the analytical study design involves analyzing the 99
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	samples in a randomized way, with 16 QC and 9 EQC inserted every 6 and 12 runs
SP:SAMPLEPREP_SUMMARY            	respectively to assess analytical precision. Subsequently, 18 identification
